How to read this
  • Each card is one side of one market. A green Yes means these traders bet that it happens. A red No means they bet it doesn’t.
  • The row of bars is the group of top earners we track. A colored bar means that trader holds this bet. More colored bars means more agreement.
  • Put in is what they paid. Worth now is what it’s worth at today’s price. The percentage shows whether they’re currently up or down.
  • Avg buy price and Price now are in cents, and 55¢ means the market thinks there’s about a 55% chance. If the price now is higher than what they paid, the market has moved their way.
  • Click a card to see which traders hold it and how much each has in.
