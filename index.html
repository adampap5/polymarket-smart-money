How to read this
  • Each row is one side of one market. YES means they bet it happens, NO means they don’t.
  • Top traders is how many tracked earners hold that side.
  • Worth now is today’s value. The percentage compares it to what they put in.
  • Price is in cents: 55¢ means roughly a 55% chance. Click a row to see who holds it.
  • Top picks ranks ideas by a conviction score. My bets is your own log, saved only in this browser.
  • The Kalshi button opens the matching Kalshi market when one is found, otherwise a search for it.
  • The Bet buttons open that market on Polymarket. You place the bet there with your own account. Hover over a button, or tap the ? beside it, for a plain-English explanation.
